Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about listings, accounts, locations, inquiries, reviews, and caretaker support.

⌕

General questions

What is Masitech Solutions?

Masitech Solutions is a platform for discovering student boarding houses and private apartments in different locations.

Who can use the platform?

Students can browse student accommodation, while non-student tenants can browse apartments. Visitors can view public listings, while an account may be required for inquiries, reviews, or other features.

Which locations are available?

Available locations are shown in the location selector and listing filters. The owner or administrator can add, edit, or remove locations as the accommodation network changes.

Does the platform guarantee accommodation?

No. The platform displays accommodation information and helps users make inquiries. Availability, approval, pricing, viewing arrangements, and tenancy agreements must be confirmed with the relevant property representative.

Listings and properties

How do I find a student boarding house?

Select the student listings page, choose a location, and use the search or sorting options to compare available boarding houses.

How do I find an apartment?

Open the apartments page, select your preferred location, and compare available apartments using the search and sorting controls.

What information is shown on a listing?

A listing may show photos, property name, location, price, room or unit type, condition, amenities, availability, ratings, reviews, and caretaker contact information.

Why can a listing disappear?

A listing may be hidden because it is occupied, reserved, under maintenance, outdated, awaiting approval, or removed by an authorized administrator.

Can I trust every detail in a listing?

Users should confirm important details directly with the relevant caretaker or property representative. Prices, condition, availability, and amenities may change.

Accounts and login

Do I need an account to browse listings?

Public listings may be browsed without an account. An account may be required to submit inquiries, save properties, submit reviews, or access dashboard features.

What account types can I create?

Public registration is available for students and non-student tenants. Caretaker and administrator accounts should be created or approved by authorized administrators.

What if I forget my password?

Use the Forgot Password page and enter the email address connected to your account.

Why do I need to select a location?

Location selection helps organize listings and show accommodation information relevant to the campus or area where you want to live.

Inquiries and tenancy

How do I contact a caretaker?

Open the property details page and use the available inquiry or contact option. You may need to log in before submitting an inquiry.

Does submitting an inquiry reserve a room?

No. An inquiry is a request for information or communication. It does not automatically reserve a room, apartment, or bed.

Where do I confirm rent and availability?

Confirm current rent, availability, deposits, viewing times, house rules, and tenancy requirements with the relevant caretaker or authorized property representative.

Does the platform process rental payments?

Payment functionality depends on the features enabled by the owner. Unless the platform clearly states otherwise, do not assume that the platform processes rent, deposits, or booking payments.

Reviews and ratings

Who can submit a review?

Registered users may submit reviews where the feature is enabled. The platform may later require evidence of an inquiry, application, or tenancy before accepting a review.

Can a caretaker edit my review?

No. Caretakers should not edit tenant reviews. They may be allowed to respond, while administrators may moderate content that violates platform rules.

What should I include in a review?

Reviews should be honest, relevant, respectful, and based on your genuine experience. Do not publish private information, threats, spam, or deliberately false claims.

Caretaker questions

What does a caretaker do?

A caretaker may manage assigned properties, update availability, maintain listing details, respond to inquiries, and report operational issues to administrators.

Can a caretaker manage every location?

No. Caretaker access should be limited to the locations and properties assigned to that caretaker by an authorized administrator.

Can caretakers edit tenant reviews?

No. Caretakers should not edit or delete tenant reviews. Review moderation should be controlled by authorized administrators.

Privacy and security

How is my information used?

Information may be used to manage your account, show relevant listings, process inquiries, provide support, improve the platform, and protect against misuse. Read the Privacy Policy for more information.

How can I request help with my information?

Contact the platform using the details on the Contact page and describe your request.

How do I report suspicious activity?

Contact the platform promptly with the listing, account, or message details. Do not send money or share sensitive information until the matter has been investigated.
